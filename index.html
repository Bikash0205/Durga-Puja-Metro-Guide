Official Metro Railway Notice:

Kolkata Metro (CRIS / Indian Railways) operates on fixed master timetables and does not provide a public live GPS/GTFS streaming API. Our real-time countdown synchronizes with Metro Railway Kolkata's officially gazetted 2026 Durga Puja Special Timetable.

284 Services

• Panchami & Shashthi: 06:00 AM to 11:00 PM (Last train 11:00 PM from both ends).

• Saptami to Navami: Continuous all-night service from 1:00 PM till 04:00 AM next morning.

• Peak Evening Headway: Every 6 minutes (6:00 PM to 02:00 AM).

• Late Night Headway: Every 12 minutes (02:00 AM to 04:00 AM).
