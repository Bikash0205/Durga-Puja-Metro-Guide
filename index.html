Kolkata Metro Puja 2026 Timings

Blue Line: Dakshineswar ⇄ Kavi Subhash

• Saptami to Navami: All-night metro operates continuously until 4:00 AM.

• Peak Frequency: 6 to 7 minutes during evenings and late night.

Green Line (Underwater): Howrah Maidan ⇄ Sector V

• Cross the Hooghly underwater from Howrah Station to Esplanade & Salt Lake in minutes.

• Services extended till midnight during peak Puja nights.

Orange & Purple Lines

• Orange Line: Kavi Subhash ⇄ Beleghata (connects EM Bypass pandals).

• Purple Line: Joka ⇄ Majerhat (direct access for Suruchi Sangha & Behala).

💡 Pro Tips for Night Hopping:

  • Purchase a rechargeable Smart Card or use the Metro Ride Kolkata QR ticketing app to bypass token counter queues.
  • Esplanade Station is the core cross-city interchange between Blue and Green Lines.
  • Carry water and light snacks; Kolkata walking routes average 2.5–4.5 km per circuit.
