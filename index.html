Kolkata Metro Puja 2026 Schedule

Blue Line (North-South): Dakshineswar ⇄ Kavi Subhash

• Saptami to Navami: Trains operate continuously overnight until 4:00 AM.

• Peak Frequency: 6 to 8 minutes during evenings and late night.

Green Line (Underwater): Howrah Maidan ⇄ Sector V

• Connects Howrah Railway Station under the river to Esplanade & Salt Lake.

• Operates till midnight during peak Puja days with 10-12 min intervals.

Orange & Purple Lines

• Orange Line: Kavi Subhash ⇄ Beleghata (EM Bypass connection).

• Purple Line: Joka ⇄ Majerhat (Behala connection).

💡 Essential Pandal Hopping Metro Tips:

  • Buy Metro Smart Card or use the official Metro Ride QR app to avoid long ticket queues.
  • Esplanade Station is the primary interchange between Blue and Green Lines.
  • Keep water and a portable power bank; North & South Kolkata walking routes average 3–5 km per circuit.
