Bonedi Bari Visiting Etiquette & Sacred Protocols

⏰ Sacred Visiting Hours (Active Ancestral Residences):

Unlike public theme pandals open all night, Bonedi Baris are private family homes. Best visited during 8:00 AM – 1:00 PM (Morning Puja & Pushpanjali) and 4:30 PM – 8:30 PM (Sandhya Arati & Dhunuchi Naach). Courtyards close between 1:30 PM – 4:00 PM for Bhog preparation.

🥿 Footwear & Sanctum Sanctity:

Please remove footwear before stepping onto carpeted Thakurdalan areas. Strictly avoid stepping onto the consecrated raised sanctum altar (বেদী / Vedi) where family priests conduct rituals.

📸 Photography Rules:

Courtyard photography is welcomed, but flash photography is strictly forbidden. Never block priests or family members during offering rituals.

✨ Unique Rituals to Witness:

• Shovabazar: Cannon firing to mark the onset of Sandhi Puja.
• Dorjeepara Mitra Bari: Unique horse-faced lion (Ashwamukhi Singha).
• Thanthania Dutta Bari & Laha Bari: Peaceful Hara-Gauri posture without Mahishasura.
• Mallick Bari: Sacred release of Nilkantha birds on Dashami.
