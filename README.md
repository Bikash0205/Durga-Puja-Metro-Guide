# Kolkata Durga Puja 2026 — Metro & Pedestrian Walking Guide

An exclusive mobile-first web app & walking circuit guide for pandal hoppers across Kolkata.

Drop off at any Kolkata Metro station and walk through all nearby pandals step-by-step with real-time turn-by-turn Google Maps navigation (Walking & Driving).

---

## ✨ What's New in the 2026 Edition

1. **Pedestrian Walking Circuits (Drop & Hop):**
   - **South Mega Walk (Kalighat & Gariahat):** Kalighat Metro $\rightarrow$ Badamtala Ashar Sangha $\rightarrow$ 66 Pally $\rightarrow$ Nepal Bhattacharjee St $\rightarrow$ Deshapriya Park $\rightarrow$ Tridhara $\rightarrow$ Samaj Sebi $\rightarrow$ Singhi Park $\rightarrow$ Ekdalia Evergreen.
   - **North Heritage Walk (Shyambazar & Sovabazar):** Bagbazar Sarbojanin $\rightarrow$ Jagat Mukherjee Park $\rightarrow$ Kumartuli Park $\rightarrow$ Kumartuli Sarbojanin $\rightarrow$ Ahiritola Jubak Brinda.
   - **North Art Trail (Hatibagan & Kashi Bose Lane):** Sikdar Bagan $\rightarrow$ Hatibagan Nabinpally $\rightarrow$ Kashi Bose Lane $\rightarrow$ Nalin Sarkar Street.
   - **Central & Underwater Link:** Central Metro $\rightarrow$ Mohammad Ali Park $\rightarrow$ College Square $\rightarrow$ Santosh Mitra Square (Sealdah Green Line).
   - **Artistic Vanguard Trail:** Belgachia Metro $\rightarrow$ Belgachia Sadharan $\rightarrow$ Tala Prattoy (*"Biyojon"* 2026 Theme).
   - **Dum Dum Park Trio:** Dum Dum Park Bharat Chakra $\rightarrow$ Dum Dum Park Sarbojanin $\rightarrow$ Dum Dum Park Tarun Sangha.
   - **Salt Lake Green Line Walk:** Karunamoyee $\rightarrow$ FD Block $\rightarrow$ BJ Block $\rightarrow$ Central Park.

2. **One-Tap Google Maps Integration:**
   - **Multi-Stop Walking Circuit:** One click generates the entire multi-point walking trail directly inside Google Maps.
   - **Point-to-Point Hops:** When inspecting any pandal, tap **"Walk"** or **"Drive"** to nearby neighboring pandals with automatic waypoint navigation.

3. **Curated 2026 Themes & Pandal Directory:**
   - Detailed theme updates for 2026 (e.g. *Sreebhumi Sporting Club's Hawa Mahal*, *Suruchi Sangha's Lotus Theme*, *Tala Prattoy's Biyojon*, *Ekdalia Evergreen Golden Temple*, etc.).
   - Full search by pandal name, neighborhood, metro line, and zone.

4. **"My Hop List" Custom Itinerary Builder:**
   - Bookmark pandals while browsing and generate your personalized multi-stop walking route link.

5. **2026 Night Metro Schedule & Guidelines:**
   - Information on Blue Line, Green Line underwater stretch, Orange Line (Beleghata link), and Purple Line extended night services during Puja.
